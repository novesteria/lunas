The contract formed when a consumer in Türkiye buys a Lunas plan or scans online. Polar is the seller of record; Novesteria provides the service.

1. PartiesSummary

The consumer named at checkout; Polar Software Inc. as seller of record; Novesteria, Inc. as the provider of the Lunas service.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
2. SubjectSummary

Sale of a digital service: a Lunas plan, Team seats, or pay-as-you-go Full scans, as selected at checkout.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
3. Price, payment and renewalSummary

The price shown at checkout in USD, VAT as a separate line where it applies. Monthly plans renew each period until cancelled from Settings › Plan.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
4. Delivery and performanceSummary

The service is available in the account as soon as payment is confirmed. Scans are run on request within the quota of the plan or pack.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
5. Right of withdrawalSummary

14 days from the contract date, except for the period in which the service was used with the consumer's express request for immediate access.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
6. Consumer's obligationsSummary

To scan only code the consumer has the right to scan and to keep account access to one person.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
7. DisputesSummary

Consumer arbitration committees and consumer courts at the consumer's place of residence in Türkiye.

Full text · to come from counsel (Oguz Law or CBC Law). Do not publish before review.
