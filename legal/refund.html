A 14-day refund on the first payment of a plan, in full, no questions. Ask by email or from Settings › Billing.

A renewal that has started is not refunded. Cancel before the renewal date; the plan runs to the end of the paid period and does not renew.

A single scan or a pack of five is refunded only if no scan from it has run. Used scans are not refunded. Packs expire 45 days after purchase.
