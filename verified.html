What a page shows

The verdict, the commit, the time, the four check groups, and the recent scan history. Refreshed on every scan.

What it never shows

Code, file names, the individual checks, or anything from the sandbox log. A page is proof of a verdict, not a window into the repository.

Who controls it

The project owner turns it on or off from the badge card. Off means the badge still works but the link asks for a sign-in.
